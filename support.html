Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

New installs get every Pro feature free for 21 days, with nothing to sign up for. After that, WellPad keeps working on the free tier: 3 lookups a day, up to 5 favorites and 2 saved access points. Favorites you saved during the trial are kept.

What does WellPad Pro cost?

On iPhone, WellPad Pro is a one-time purchase. There's no subscription and nothing to cancel. It adds unlimited lookups, full AER well registry details, photos on notes, unlimited favorites and access points, Update LSD Location, and backup and restore.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back on a new or reset iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought it with, open Settings → WellPad Pro → Upgrade to WellPad PRO, and tap Restore Purchase.

How do I move my favorites, notes and photos to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings → Share Backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as iCloud Drive, Files or email. On the new phone, open Settings → Restore Backup and choose that file. Backups from WellPad on Android can be restored on iPhone too. Backup and restore are Pro features.

Navigate shows a pin, but no route starts

That's by design. Navigate drops a pin on the LSD in Google Maps or Apple Maps so you can choose your route, then start it in the map app. You can pick which map app to use in Settings → Navigation. Map apps need a connection to load the map and plan the route, unless you've downloaded offline maps for the area.

GPS lookup says location access is off

On iPhone, open the Settings app → WellPad → Location and choose While Using the App. Turn on Precise Location for the best results.

How do I get the latest well data?

WellPad comes with the AER well registry built in. To download a newer copy, tap Check for Updates in Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Your data is stored only on your phone. See how to delete your data.
