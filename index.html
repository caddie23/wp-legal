Privacy Policy

WellPad: Alberta LSD Lookup

Developer: Southpaw Digital Labs Inc.
Effective Date: January 7, 2026
Last Updated: September 30, 2026

The short version: There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your favorites, history, notes, photos and corrections stay on your device. A few features need the internet: live weather sends the parcel's coordinates to a weather service, well registry updates are downloaded from our data server, purchases go through Google Play, and crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. We never sell your data.

Overview

WellPad: Alberta LSD Lookup ("WellPad", "the App") is developed and operated by Southpaw Digital Labs Inc. ("Company", "we", "us"). WellPad is designed with privacy as a core principle. This privacy policy explains what information the App uses, what leaves your device, and why.

Information We Do NOT Collect

WellPad does not collect:

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Accounts or logins of any kind
  • Your search history or lookup data
  • Your favorites, notes, photos, or corrections
  • Usage analytics, advertising IDs, or behavioral data

We have no servers that receive your personal data.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device:

Android Backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may back up your favorites, history, notes, corrections and settings to your own Google account, and copy them (with photos) when you transfer to a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings. We cannot access these backups.

Location Access

WellPad asks for location permission for two features:

  • GPS lookup (the location button on the Lookup screen): finds the parcel you're standing in
  • Update LSD Location: saves your current position as a corrected wellhead location

Your GPS position is used on your device and stored only locally (in History and Corrections). WellPad does not send your GPS position to us or anyone else. You can deny location permission and still use every other feature.

When Live Lease Weather is on, the weather request contains the parcel's coordinates (see below). After a GPS lookup, those are the coordinates of the parcel you're in, so they are close to where you are.

Features That Use the Internet

Live Lease Weather (Open-Meteo)

  • What is sent: the latitude and longitude of the parcel you're viewing, and your device's IP address (as with any internet request)
  • When: each time you open a parcel's details, while Live Lease Weather is turned on (it is on by default)
  • Why: to show current temperature, wind and precipitation at the lease
  • Turn it off: Settings → Live Lease Weather
  • Privacy policy: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms

We don't send any identifier or other information with the request.

Well Registry Updates (GitHub)

  • What is sent: a download request (including your IP address) to our public data repository hosted on GitHub
  • When: only when you tap Check for Updates in Settings
  • Why: to download the latest AER well registry data
  • Privacy policy: GitHub General Privacy Statement

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases of WellPad Pro are processed entirely by Google Play. The app asks Google Play which purchases your signed-in Google account owns, to unlock Pro. We never see your payment details. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Crash Reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

What is sent when the app crashes: the crash stack trace, device model, manufacturer and Android version, app version, and a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics to count crashes. It is not linked to your name, email, or Google account.

What is NOT sent: your location, lookups, favorites, history, notes, photos, or anything you type.

Privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

External Map Applications

When you tap "Navigate" or "Share", WellPad hands the parcel's coordinates to the map or sharing app you choose. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Data Security

  • Data on your device is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandboxing
  • All internet requests use encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by the app on your device

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. See how to delete your data.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics under Google's retention policy. To ask us to delete crash reports from your device, email us.
  • Weather and update requests: not stored by us. Open-Meteo and GitHub handle their own server logs.

Children's Privacy

WellPad does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for professional field workers and does not target children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be noted with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
